About
EVOKE

A conference for delegates who question, debate and engage beyond the obvious.

EVOKE MUN 2026 is a student-led Model United Nations conference built to create a space for discussion, diplomacy, strategy and challenging perspectives.

Designed for those who question, debate and engage beyond the obvious, EVOKE brings together delegates in a conference environment where ideas are tested, positions are challenged and diplomacy takes centre stage.

The conference is designed around meaningful committee experiences, strong Executive Boards and an engaging environment for delegates.
